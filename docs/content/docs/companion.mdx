---
title: iOS and Vision Pro companion
description: Connect your iPhone, iPad, or Vision Pro to projects and AI tools on Desktop.
---

Use Glowbom on **iPhone, iPad, or Vision Pro** to capture ideas, chat, review
builds, and browse media. Desktop runs the tools and keeps your project files.

The companion endpoints remain in the open backend. Pairing and creation
screens in this guide belong to the separate Desktop and native app clients,
not the public Agent + Buzz browser.

The companion is a **development preview requiring matching current builds**.
Older published apps may not include it. See [Desktop availability](./desktop).

## Before connecting

Keep Desktop open and your computer awake on the same Wi-Fi or local network
as your device. Allow **Local Network** access when asked. Set up and sign in to
your [Desktop AI tools](./connect-ai) first. Cloud models need internet access.

Open the projects you want to share on Desktop before enabling the connection.

## Pair with Desktop

1. On Desktop, open **Settings > Phone connection**.
2. Choose your local network address if asked, then select **Enable phone
   connection**. Keep the panel open.
3. In the companion's **Home**, choose **Find Desktop** and select your computer.
4. Compare the six-digit code on both screens. Choose **The codes match** on your
   device, then **Codes match, approve** on Desktop to complete pairing.

Nearby pairing stays open for five minutes. Choose **Open nearby pairing** on
Desktop to reopen it.

You can also choose **Copy connection code** on Desktop. On your device, expand
**Use a connection code instead**, paste the complete code, and
choose **Connect to Desktop**. Supported iPhone and iPad devices can also choose
**Scan a QR code**. Vision Pro uses nearby pairing or the copied code.

## Use the same projects

- **Chat:** choose **No project** for an idea, or a shared project for its saved
  conversation. Chat does not change files or start builds.
- **Create project:** start with words, a drawing, or a photo. Review the name,
  Desktop folder, model, and optional image provider before choosing **Create
  project**. The first version is saved on your computer.
- **Build:** review the instructions, model, target platforms, and attachments.
  Choose from Desktop's connected agents, including configured ACP agents.
  Follow progress and answer supported questions or permissions from either
  device. Cursor, Claude Code, and ACP appear in **Build**, not general Chat.
- **Project:** read its [Project Book](./project-book), rename it, change its icon,
  preview it, or open it in a Desktop editor. Book editing stays on Desktop.
- **Studio:** browse shared projects, images, videos, and audio. Create images or
  videos through connected Desktop providers. Play and share saved music, voice,
  and sounds; audio generation stays on Desktop.

Vision Pro's **Take a photo on Vision Pro** guides you through Capture with the
top button, then attaching the result from Photos. Direct camera capture and QR
scanning require supported iOS devices.

## Access, models, and charges

Credentials stay on Desktop. Messages and media go through it to your selected
provider. Model access, limits, and charges depend on that provider. Project
review can send your written idea to a model to suggest a name.

Pairing uses an encrypted connection and verifies Desktop's certificate. Keep codes
private: they grant access to shared projects, chats, builds, approvals, and media
creation. Access expires after eight hours. **Stop phone connection** revokes it
for all paired devices; accepted code builds continue. Use the build's **Stop**
action to cancel one.

Build permissions depend on the agent. **Allow all for this build** starts off
and lasts only for that build; questions and separate paid-media reviews still
need an answer. Cursor uses its CLI's automatic mode and deny rules.

Static previews open as browser copies. Live server previews ask you to approve
a temporary, unencrypted HTTP link. Use it without real passwords or private data.

## If something is missing

Update both apps. For source builds, restart the updated backend too. Check Local
Network permission and Wi-Fi isolation; try the copied code if discovery fails.

Shared folders are selected when the connection starts. To share an additional
existing project, open it on Desktop, stop and enable **Phone connection** again,
then reconnect. Projects created through the companion are shared automatically.

Direct mobile account access and on-device creation are hidden in this preview.
