## What is Glowbom?

Glowbom is a platform that turns your sketches into real native apps on every platform.

You draw one sketch and it builds a real iPhone app, a real Android app, and a full web app. Then coding agents make it production ready on your own computer. The local build workflow, [Glowbom OSS](./glowbom-oss), is free and open source.

## Get started

1. Go to [glowbom.com](https://glowbom.com), sketch your idea, and press **Build**.
2. Play with the prototype in your browser.
3. Pick a platform: iOS, Android, or web.
4. Use [Glowbom OSS](./glowbom-oss) to make it production ready.

See the [Quickstart](./quickstart) for a step-by-step walkthrough with screenshots.

## Models and providers

Glowbom OSS is powered by your local OpenCode setup. We recommend signing in to ChatGPT through OpenCode, but you can use any provider and model available in your OpenCode configuration.
